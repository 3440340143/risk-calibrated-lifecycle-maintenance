# Result and field dictionary

The authoritative numeric snapshot is in `数据/`. See `字段索引.csv` for all filenames, row counts and exact column names. A field's grain is determined by its file, not its name alone.

## Sampling keys and splits

`scenario`: S-FD001--S-FD004, synthetic operating/failure configurations. `seed`: 11,22,33,44,55. `unit_id` is unique only within a scenario/seed fleet. Fit IDs 1--60, tune 61--90, calibration 91--130, test 131--180. The main test wide file has 1,000 distinct scenario/seed/unit keys. S-FD004-specific controls generally contain 250 test units. `method`, `policy`, `variant`, `mode` and `stage` identify the indicated experiment, not interchangeable populations.

## Physical events and costs

- `T_u`: natural failure cycle, known in synthetic run-to-failure replay. It is unobserved after successful preventive replacement in deployment.
- `*_tau`: first alarm cycle; negative sentinel -1 means no alarm (mathematical infinity). The emergency backup can use tau=0.
- `*_fail`, `n_fails`, `fail_pct`: event `Z = 1{tau + ell >= T_u}`, its count, or 100 times its mean. Default ell=15 cycles. Equality is a failure.
- Realized service `U=min(T_u,tau+ell)`. Unused life `W=max(0,T_u-(tau+ell))`; W is not observed after successful field replacement.
- `*_cost`, `unit_cost`: normalized lifecycle cost. Default preventive cost 1, breakdown cost rho=10, plus 0.045 per unused cycle for successful prevention. Costs are not field-validated currency.
- `j_life_rate`: 100 * sum(cost) / sum(U), per 100 useful operating cycles. Its `*_mean` is normally the mean over scenario--seed or seed fleet ratios, not the ratio pooled over all units.
- `j_event_rate`: event-cost rate omitting the wasted-life component.
- `avail_pct` / `avail_pct_mean`: useful-life utilization 100*sum(U)/sum(T_u); these field names do not measure availability with an explicit downtime model.
- `waste_cycles`: mean unused cycles over evaluated units.

## Predictions, margins and uncertainty

`rmse`, `rmse_all`, `rmse_near35`, `rmse_mid*`: remaining-life prediction error over the named cycle subset. Training RUL is capped at 125 where specified; raw natural remaining life and capped labels must not be interchanged.

`lambda_tuned`, `lambda_unit_pure`, `lambda_static`: cost-tuned margin, pure unit-order-statistic margin, and deployed static combined margin. `tuning_floor_active` records whether the corresponding floor comparison is active. `lambda` is in standardized score units where scale is used. Cycle residual thresholds and standardized unit margins must not be compared numerically without their scales.

`B1`--`B10`: uncalibrated threshold; Ridge cost-tuned PTO; smooth pinball+residual CP; NASA-loss+split CP; one-sided cycle residual CP; cycle ACI; in-house Linex smooth-cost tuning; Ridge pure unit CP; static Linex unit policy; online event unit policy. Consult the source for the complete fitted/post-processing configuration.

`Q1*`: matched four-way margin controls; `L1`--`L4`: matched losses; `A0`--`A6` and A2_online: component/policy controls. A2 variants are policy replacements, not isolated removals of one module. `C1`--`C5`: oracle, censored-imputation, failures-only, static unit, and observable-event feedback.

## Diagnostic and statistical fields

`cost_eff100` and `cost_direct10` in the D0--D5 forensic audit are legacy per-cycle proxy costs with effective cost ratio 100 or direct ratio 10. They are not lifecycle stopping cost rates.

`mean_difference`, `ci95_low`, `ci95_high`: paired differences and indicated t confidence intervals. `p_ttest_20*` is based on 20 scenario--seed pairs; `p_ttest_5seedblock*` uses five independent seed blocks. Holm-adjusted and Wilcoxon fields are identified in their column names. With five pairs a two-sided exact signed-rank p-value has a minimum of 0.0625. Intervals crossing zero do not establish superiority. Pooled Clopper--Pearson UCB columns are descriptive under shared calibration/model dependence.

## Mechanism mismatch and timing

Per-mode refit and frozen-source transfer are separate protocols. The legacy `unseen_regime` changes additional RNG-driven parameters and is a compound shift. `unseen_regime_isolated` preserves known-regime parameters and test lifetimes, isolates the new regime intervention, and does not separately identify normalization-fallback effects.

Running-max ON/OFF refit branches preserve architecture/hyperparameters but refit coefficients and recalibrate thresholds. The test-time OFF-only branch freezes the exact ON coefficients and margin. Recovery-mask RMSE and whole-life cost are different estimands.

CPU files contain original recorded elapsed time units in their headers: batch microseconds/cycle, streaming milliseconds or raw seconds as named, direct per-unit batch replay, and offline Fit+Tune+Cal. Four architectures each use 25 complete trajectories (5,251 steps) for streaming and 250 units for direct replay. Across architectures the raw files have 21,004 step rows and 1,000 unit rows. These timings do not describe this Windows preparation run.

`results/pdf_layout_audit.csv` refers only to the prior 15-page manuscript. The new ESWA PDF audit is `../04_Preflight_Audit/PDF_Layout_Audit_ESWA.csv`.


## Empirical physical benchmarks and economic-gain regimes

- `nist_apr423_arrhenius_degradation_raw.csv`: Verbatim NIST/SEMATECH Section 8.4.2.3 physical component degradation table across 3 temperature stress regimes (65C, 85C, 105C; 15 physical components, 45 readouts at 200, 500, 1000 hr; failure threshold D_F=30.0%) and log-linear extrapolated failure hours.
- `nist_mpc61_wafer_resistivity_raw.csv`: Verbatim NIST/SEMATECH Section 2.6.1.1.1 (`MPC61.DAT`) longitudinal silicon wafer resistivity gauge readings (300 physical measurements across 5 check-standard wafers #138--#142, 5 probes, and 2 chronological runs in Month 3 and Month 4; 50 series of six ordered measurements).
- `nist_empirical_benchmark_per_split.csv` and `nist_empirical_benchmark_summary.csv`: Split-level (125 exhaustive leave-one-triple-out splits on NIST Arrhenius; 20 chronological Run 1 -> Run 2 folds on NIST MPC61) and summary lifecycle cost rate (`j_life_mean`), breakdown rate (`fail_pct`), unused life (`waste_cycles`), and descriptive split differences against ERM and Cycle-CP. Repeated splits are not independent physical replicates; these NIST summaries do not report cross-population p-values or deployment risk certificates.
- `tuning_sample_scarcity_per_seed.csv` and `tuning_sample_scarcity_summary.csv`: Controlled tuning-split size sweep (`n_tune` in {6, 10, 15, 20, 30}) across 1,000 test trajectories (4 scenarios x 5 seeds) comparing ERM cost-tuned margin (`j_erm`) against DC-PdM (`j_dcpdm`, cost-tuned + unit-level conformal floor).
- `commissioning_to_field_per_seed.csv` and `commissioning_to_field_summary.csv`: Commissioning-to-field regime shift across 1,000 field test trajectories where Fit/Tune splits come from clean commissioning runs (`no_shocks`) and Cal/Test splits come from field runs (`neg_dips`).
